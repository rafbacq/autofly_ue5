# 2026-10-08 — C4: s03 scattered rocks and s06 tree clusters, built, packaged and gated

Plan 5's order (U3) after s09, s07 and s02 was s06 (trees) and the rock scenes. Both were designed offline, built in
the editor, packaged with the other maps, looked at through the drone's cameras and gated live, in one morning while
the GPU was free. Records: `docs/gates/m4_s03_gate.json`, `docs/gates/m4_s06_gate.json`; figure
`results/2026-10-07-project-status/figures/layouts_c4.png`; sheets `results/2026-10-07-project-status/scenes/`.

## s03 scattered rocks (grass)

- **Assets and scales.** The Poly Haven boulders are 0.5–1.9 m tall at unit scale, below the flight band, so every
  group is scaled into it (fact 10 of plan 5): namaqualand_boulder_04 ×1.6–2.0 (3.0–3.8 m tall), _03 ×1.5–1.9
  (2.2–2.8 m), boulder_01 ×2.0–2.6 (2.0–2.6 m), _02 ×2.0–2.5 (1.8–2.2 m), _06 ×2.6–3.4 (1.7–2.2 m; a flat boulder,
  so the largest scale gives the shortest rocks). Poisson placement, 8 m margin, 8–12 m minimum distances.
- **Counts.** The first draft (6, 6, 8, 6, 8 = 34 rocks) generated and was reachable but its worst crossing detour was
  1.279, over U4's 1.25. An offline sweep of counts and seeds (every variant in `scenes/` terms, in process) showed the
  bound is a density bound: 29 rocks (5, 5, 7, 5, 7) at the base seed 1003 give 1.167 (per edge 1.131–1.167),
  unreachable 0, through-unreachable 0. The base seed was kept; no seed was hunted.
- **Gate.** 29 of 29 actors as built (centre error ≤ 3 µm, size ≤ 0.13 mm); depth probes on boulder_01 (obs_0000) at
  3, 6 and 12 m all passed; the crash into it collided at 7.75 m of travel against 7.53 m predicted, 1.30 m inside its
  footprint circle (the circle is the whole model's reach; the rock's surface is inside it). **The reset after that
  crash could not free the actor in eight attempts**: every retrace sweep (1.5, 4, then 8 m) was stopped at zero
  travel. So rocks wedge the swept actor as wood does, and the footprint rule, which covers every measured model, is
  what keeps the body off them. The gate does not require that reset for a mesh (run 3 of s02 set that rule); the
  record keeps the attempt list.

## s06 tree clusters (grass)

- **Design.** Single-species clusters (the generator places each group's clusters on its own, so a cluster is one
  species, like a stand): searsia_burchellii large / medium / small and searsia_lucida_c, in discs of 5–7 m radius.
- **The gate's probe needs a trunk.** The first s06 (42 bushes, detour 1.163) built, packaged and looked right, but
  its live check stopped before the first depth probe: "no unobstructed depth probe at 3.0 m". A diagnostic over
  the chooser's criteria showed every candidate pose failing on one of two counts: no solid wood in the camera's
  centre window (a searsia bush keeps its stems inside its leaf canopy, so from outside its whole-reach circle no
  opaque patch is visible at 2.0, 1.5 or 1.2 m) or the pose inside a cluster neighbour's body clearance. The depth
  check as designed measures an opaque interior patch to millimetres and bounds card pixels only from below; a
  bush-only scene offers no such patch.
- **Decision: four emergent trunk trees, not a weaker check.** s06 gained a first group of 2 tree_small_02 and 2
  island_tree_02 (poisson, 18 m apart), which stand above the clusters as emergent trees do, and the bush counts went
  to 6, 9, 9, 12 so the detour stays under the bound: 40 obstacles, detour 1.171 at the base seed 1006. The scene is
  still bushy tree clusters; the taller trees give it a vertical variety the paper's fig. 8 scene has anyway. A
  bush-only scene (s06r reuses this file, so not that one) would need a canopy probe: a pose whose wide window holds
  enough predicted card pixels, checked for "no pixel nearer than its card" and for a pre-registered share of card
  pixels matching their prediction. Left for when a scene needs it.
- **Gate (second run, `m4_s06_gate.json`).** 80 of 80 actors found (40 boxed within 2 µm, the 40 cards actors present
  without a box, as on s02); probes on island_tree_02 (obs_0002) at 3 m and tree_small_02 (obs_0003) at 6 and 8 m
  (the 12 m probe fell back, as on s02); the crash into obs_0003's wood at 9.64 m against 9.48 m predicted; the reset
  freed by a 1.5 m retrace on its first attempt. The record's 8.6 GB of VRAM is the host's total: the s02 smoke
  training's five simulators were running beside the gate's.

## Pipeline timings (for planning the remaining scenes)

Offline design and sweep: minutes. Editor build and verify: about a minute per level (warm derived-data cache).
Packaging: about a minute incrementally (the binary's hash is unchanged, so `package_matches_manifest` holds; the pak
changes, and `runs/package/package_manifest.json` is regenerated after each packaging). Snapshots: 2 minutes per
scene. Gate: 8 minutes per scene. So a scene whose assets are imported costs under half an hour of live time, and
only packaging needs every simulator stopped.
