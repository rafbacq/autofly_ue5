## Run 4

boundary (`2026-10-04-s01d-r4-clearance-penalty.md`). It was stopped cleanly on request at 359,900 steps (7.5 h) on

### Why it was stopped, and what that got wrong

It was stopped for two reasons:
- **The eval watch's 20-episode scores slid after 200k:** 0.85, 0.70, 0.65, 0.40. The 350k checkpoint climbed out of
  the altitude band in 9 of its 20 episodes.
- **That climb looked like a reward gap** that more steps would not close.

Selection then showed that **the slide was not real**. On 40 validation episodes, run 4's late checkpoints were its
best:

| Checkpoint | 160k | 180k | 200k | 220k | 240k | 260k | 280k | 300k | 320k | 340k | final (359.9k) |

Twenty episodes cannot rank checkpoints; the runbook and MEMORY.md already said so. The stop cost run 4 the 4.5 h left

## Run 5: run 4 plus two margins

`runs/expert/s01d_r5` is run 4's exact command with two training-only additions (runbook §6d):

1. **`--mover-contact-margin 0.3`** (7fcb8a8). A training episode ends as a mover collision 1.3 m from the surface,
   not the task's 1.0 m.
   - **What stays the task's:** the observation (clearance to the 1.0 m rule), the yield rule and the gate. So what
     the expert sees is bit-identical between training and evaluation, and only where a training episode ends moves.
   - **Why 0.3 m:** the largest overshoot seen in any run's evaluation is 0.24 m (a contact at 0.76 m).
   - **The trade-off:** a wider training boundary closes some narrow passages in training. Static pillars need about
     0.47 m (the rotor tips), so a mover in a typical 6 m corridor still leaves room on either side.
2. **`--altitude-margin-penalty 0.1 0.5`** (362751b). The cost is 0.1 per step at the band's floor or ceiling, falling
   to 0 at 0.5 m inside it.
   - **Why:** the task's reward does not care where the drone is inside the band until it leaves it, and every s01d
     expert lost episodes to slow climbs or dives: r1's 10 `altitude_high` gate exits and its diving final, r4's 160k
     (38 of 40) and 350k (9 of 20).
   - **Cost to the policy:** holding the middle of the band is free, and success does not depend on altitude, so the
     penalty guides without costing anything else.

The closing penalty (b40163e) is built and tested but **not used**: run 5 changes only what the evidence points at.

**What would show it worked:**
- mover-contact gaps in training landing between 1.0 and 1.3 m, the margin at work;
- stage-1 and stage-2 scores above run 4's (0.825 and 0.78);
- ≥ 0.95 at the gate.

**Against run 1's best_model on the same 200 episodes** (0.775, CI 0.712–0.827; `runs/m2d_diag/paired_compare.py`):
